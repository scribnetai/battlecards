FAQ

The fine print, up front.

What is a battlecard, exactly?

A competitive cheat sheet for presales: the positioning, discovery questions, trap questions, and objection handling you need when a deal turns into vendor A vs. vendor B. Built for SE prep — not customer collateral.

Why three pages instead of one comparison table?

A table tells you who wins a feature. It doesn't tell you what the other rep will say in the room. Pages 2 and 3 argue each side's best case so you walk in knowing both scripts — including the traps aimed at your vendor.

How current is the information?

Every card shows a "last verified" date. Specs, pricing, and licensing change fast in this industry — verify against current vendor datasheets before any customer conversation. Vendor claims are labeled as claims.

Could a vendor take issue with these comparisons?

These cards are built from public vendor materials — datasheets, published pricing, analyst reports, and the vendors' own marketing claims. Competitive claims are explicitly labeled as claims ("Dell's claim"), every card carries a "last verified" date, and nothing here is presented as a vendor's official position. That's the same standard as any competitive cheat sheet an SE builds by hand. If you spot something outdated or misattributed, flag it and it gets corrected.

Can I send a battlecard to a customer?

No — don't. These are internal prep tools: trap questions and objection handling lose their value (and gain risk) outside the team. Use what you learn here to run a better meeting; keep the card itself internal.

Are the pricing and licensing numbers guaranteed accurate?

No. They're researched snapshots, accurate as of the card's "last verified" date. Vendor pricing and licensing shift constantly — treat every number as a starting point and verify against the current datasheet and your distributor quote before repeating it in a customer room.

Can I request a matchup?

Yes — new cards get added to the index as they're built. Ask Sparky for the matchup you need.

Is any of my data collected?

No. Like the other SE tools, this app is 100% client-side. Nothing you view or click leaves your browser.
